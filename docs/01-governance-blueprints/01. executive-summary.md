## 🏛️ LEVEL 1: BENEFICIARIES, CASE STUDY & SUSTAINABILITY IMPACT (The Context)

Level 1 establishes the localized context of the bottleneck, identifying the core users, customers, and environmental penalties.

* **Geographic Case Study & Sustainability Relevance (Why Dorohusk?):**
  * *High Traffic Intensity:* Dorohusk handles a baseline volume of **1,200 heavy goods vehicles (HGVs) per day** (~438,000 trucks/year), where queue delays routinely stretch up to **42.5 hours**.
  * *Cold-Chain Vulnerability:* A high proportion of transit consists of temperature-sensitive food and pharma cargo. Auxiliary diesel refrigeration units (reefers) burn fuel continuously at **2.50 L/hour** during queue delays.
  * *Localized Air Quality & Carbon Footprint:* Idling engines consume uncombusted diesel emitting **2.68 kg CO2/Liter** (EEA/DEFRA standard factor), severely degrading local air quality with high **PM2.5** and **NOx** concentrations for adjacent communities.
* **Defined Users & Customers:**
  * **Primary First Customer (Who Pays):** Sovereign Transport Ministries / Customs Border Agencies via B2G SaaS licensing (`R.2` Annual SaaS) funded through trade modernization budgets.
  * **Primary End-User (Who Operates):** Checkpoint Border Control Officers viewing the real-time WebGL SCADA Digital Twin control room interface.
  * **Indirect Beneficiaries:** Freight operators, 3PL dispatchers (recovered driver duty hours), public authorities (road safety/transparency), local border communities (reduced particulate emissions), and broader society (reduced Scope 3 emissions).

## 💻 LEVEL 2: THE SOFTWARE SOLUTION & DATA ARCHITECTURE (The Boundary)

Level 2 details the precise technical boundaries and input-process-output engine of Phase 1. This phase is an asset-light software simulation overlay and explicitly does not involve physical civil works.

* **Input $\rightarrow$ Model $\rightarrow$ Output Data Architecture:**
  1. **Operational Telemetry Inputs:** Edge telemetry ingestion (`ocr_feed`, `rfid_scan`, `wim_scales`) combined with empirical physics constants (`reefer_diesel_burn_rate_l_hr`, `t_delay_baseline`).
  2. **Sub-500ms Decision Engine:** Stateless 3-Gate verification logic (`manifest_id_hash` check, mass delta check, customs authorization) running in an isolated shadow sandbox.
  3. **Decision-Support Outputs:** 
     * *Operational:* Automated Express Pass vs. Inspection Diversion Flag.
     * *Sustainability:* Live certified tracking of saved idle hours, avoided reefer diesel burn, and prevented **CO2** emissions.
* **Risk Isolation & Passive "Shadow Mode":**
  * **Passive Shadow Sandbox:** Telemetry is passively duplicated into a Node.js sandbox environment (`sandbox/server.js`) to prevent operational disruption to live trade lanes.
  * **Zero-Knowledge Anonymization:** Cargo manifest data is anonymized via one-way SHA-256 hashing (`manifest_id_hash`) before ingestion to preserve sovereign border security.
